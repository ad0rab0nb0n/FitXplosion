Calorie Needs Information

1. Calorie Needs for Weight Loss

  • Consume 500-1000 calories less than your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE- around 1,800-2,200 calories) per day.

2. Calorie Needs for Muscle Gain

  • Consume 250-500 calories more than your TDEE (around 1,800-2,200 calories) per day.

3. Calorie Needs to Stay Fit

  • Consume calories equal to your TDEE (around 1,800-2,200 calories per day) to maintain your current weight.
